### MUI-01 · high · Every non-transaction dialog is taller than the phone viewport and cannot scroll — Save/Cancel are unreachable
- Evidence: `shots/ui/P-70-settings-add-asset.png` (Add Asset: dialog 839px tall in a 664px viewport, top edge at y=−87 so the title is off-screen, Cancel at y=704), `shots/ui/P-51-asset-addposition.png` and `P-75-campaigns-track.png` (Add interest position from Asset Detail and from Campaigns "Track": dialog box 564px but content spills — Save at y=777, Cancel y=817; on iPhone SE Save at y=818 in a 568px viewport). `components/ui/dialog.tsx:56` (`DialogContent` base has no `max-h`/`overflow`), `components/assets/AssetForm.tsx:192`, `components/interest/InterestPositionForm.tsx:215`, `components/platforms/PlatformForm.tsx:88`, `components/retirement/ScenarioNameDialog.tsx:55` (all `className="sm:max-w-md"` only). Only `AddTransactionModal.tsx:568` adds `max-h-[90vh] overflow-y-auto`.
- Why it matters: on a phone the interest-position form (a daily-use note for the owner's Enpara deposits) and the admin Add Asset form cannot be submitted at all — the buttons are below the screen and the page behind is scroll-locked.
- Proposed change: fix at the primitive — give `DialogContent` (`dialog.tsx:56`) a default `max-h-[calc(100dvh-2rem)] overflow-y-auto` (or `flex flex-col` with a scrolling body) so every dialog inherits it; on `< sm` render dialogs as bottom sheets anchored to the viewport bottom so the footer is always in thumb reach.

### MUI-02 · high · ⚠ interacts with C-02 — Add/Edit Transaction: footer hidden for all eight types on phone, and the C-02 sticky footer would take 28–33 % of the dialog
- Evidence: iPhone 14 — dialog `max-height: 597.6px` (90vh), content `scrollHeight` Buy 943 / Sell 873 / Deposit 717 / Withdrawal 787 / Dividend 965 / Interest 965 / Fee 717 / Tax 717 / asset-prefilled 967 / Edit 979; "Add Transaction" button top 848–952 vs dialog bottom 631 — off-screen for every type (Deposit's button is cut in half at 622–654). iPhone SE — dialog 511px vs content 759–1023. `shots/ui/P-41-add-Buy.png`, `P-41-add-Deposit.png`, `P-42-add-buy-scrolled.png` (footer once scrolled: three stacked full-width 32px buttons + padding ≈ 168px). `components/transactions/AddTransactionModal.tsx:568,961`; `components/ui/dialog.tsx:105` (`DialogFooter` is `flex-col-reverse` below `sm`).
- Why it matters: C-02 as ratified ("sticky footer inside `DialogContent`") is correct on desktop; on a 598px phone dialog a 168px sticky footer leaves ~400px for a 940px form, and on iPhone SE ~340px. The record-a-trade job is the one most likely to be done from a phone.
- Proposed change: implement C-02 with a phone variant — footer as one row (`Add Transaction` primary + `Save & add another` as a text/secondary button; Cancel becomes the header ✕), ~56px total; and/or render the dialog as a full-height bottom sheet (`100dvh`) below `sm`. Acceptance: submit button inside the viewport without scrolling at 375×568 for every type. Note for C-17: the Type row already wraps 4+4 at 390px (`P-41-add-Buy.png`); a ninth "Transfer" chip wraps to 4+4+1 — accept, or shorten "Withdrawal" to fit 5+4.

### MUI-03 · high · Tablet 768–1023 — the `md` breakpoint brings in the 240px sidebar and desktop tables at once; less than half of each table is visible
- Evidence: `shots/ui/T-02-portfolio.png` (table cut after "Quantity"; Bought/Price/Value/P&L/Alloc/action all off-screen), `T-03-transactions.png` (cut after "Type"). Metrics at 768×1024: `main` 528px; Portfolio table `scrollWidth` 1040 in a 480px wrapper, Transactions 972 in 480. `components/layout/Sidebar.tsx:57` (`hidden md:flex md:w-60`), `components/layout/MobileNav.tsx:32` (`md:hidden`), `components/portfolio/PortfolioTable.tsx:41,74` and `components/transactions/TransactionList.tsx:47,76` (cards `sm:hidden`, table `hidden sm:block` — i.e. tables from 640px).
- Why it matters: an iPad in portrait gets the worst of both layouts — a desktop chrome with a narrower content column than a phone card gets, and the key numbers (Value, P&L) need a horizontal drag to reach.
- Proposed change: move the sidebar/tab-bar switch to `lg` (1024) and keep the card layouts up to `lg` as well (`lg:hidden` / `hidden lg:block`), or keep `md` for the sidebar but let the tables collapse to cards whenever their wrapper is narrower than the table's natural width. ⚠ interacts with C-13: the "fit at ~990px" budget assumes a 1280 desktop; at 768 the same table has 480px and cannot be budgeted — the breakpoint move is the fix, not column widths.

### MUI-04 · medium · Bottom tab bar has no safe-area inset — labels sit under the iPhone home indicator
- Evidence: `components/layout/MobileNav.tsx:32` (`fixed bottom-0 … flex border-t`; computed `padding-bottom: 0px` on the nav and tabs), `index.html:5` (`viewport` meta without `viewport-fit=cover`), zero `safe-area`/`env(` usage anywhere in `src/`. Tabs are 98×56 with 12px labels (`MobileNav.tsx:11`, `py-2 text-xs`).
- Why it matters: on any notched iPhone the 34px home-indicator zone overlaps the 12px tab labels; the emulator cannot show it, but the CSS proves it — there is no inset to absorb it.
- Proposed change: add `viewport-fit=cover` to the meta and `pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom)]` on the nav (and the matching `pb-[calc(5rem+env(safe-area-inset-bottom))]` on `main`, `AppLayout.tsx:76`); the transaction editor footer (`TransactionsEditPage.tsx:100`) needs the same inset.

### MUI-05 · medium · Three desktop tables ship raw on phone inside a silent scroll wrapper — clipped mid-figure, key columns hidden, no cue
- Evidence: `shots/ui/07-settings-fold.png` (Settings › Assets: Price column shows "$3", "₺1", header "Pr"; Status and Actions off-screen; table 687px in 358px), Budget › Months table 570px (Spent and Savings-rate columns hidden; `shots/ui/05-budget-fold.png` metrics), Asset Detail › By platform 415–497px (`shots/ui/12-asset-thyao-fold.png` — P&L "(0.16%)" cut; `P-52-asset-try-earning.png` — "-$" of the Enpara P&L visible, rest off-screen). `components/assets/AssetList.tsx:129`, `components/budget/MonthlyBudgetTable.tsx:108`, `components/asset-detail/AssetPlatformTable.tsx:39` — plain `<Table>` in `components/ui/table.tsx:11` (`overflow-x-auto`, no shadow/fade, no sticky first column). Portfolio and Transactions already have card layouts; these three do not.
- Why it matters: the body does not scroll sideways (good), but nothing tells the user the table continues — a clipped "$3" reads as a broken number, and the row's action menu is unreachable without discovering the drag.
- Proposed change: give these three the same treatment Portfolio/Transactions got — stacked card rows below `sm` (Settings: ticker + category + price, status as a dot, actions menu; Budget: month row with Income/Invested/Spent stacked; By-platform: one row per platform with Value and P&L). At minimum: a right-edge fade on the wrapper and `position: sticky` on the first column.

### MUI-06 · medium · Above-the-fold budget — the page title is shown twice and the summary cards stack 3-high, so no holding or transaction is visible without scrolling
- Evidence: `shots/ui/02-portfolio-fold.png` (header "Portfolio" 18px + page h1 "Portfolio" 24px + subtitle; three summary cards 70/106/70px; filters; the first group header touches the fold at y≈600 and no asset row is visible), `shots/ui/03-transactions-fold.png` (same: Bulk add / Add Transaction, three 100px cards, filters — the first row is at ~y=1100 of a 664 viewport). `components/layout/Header.tsx:44` (`<h1 … md:hidden>{title}</h1>`), `pages/PortfolioPage.tsx:33`, `pages/TransactionsPage.tsx:50` (and Retirement/Budget/Campaigns/Settings — `text-2xl font-bold` h1 + subtitle, no responsive hiding), `components/portfolio/PortfolioSummaryBar.tsx:54` and `components/transactions/TransactionSummary.tsx:12` (`grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-3`).
- Why it matters: on the two most-visited pages the daily user scrolls ~450px before the first number they came for; the phone header already names the page.
- Proposed change: hide the page `h1`/subtitle below `md` (the header title does the job) and lay the summary out as one compact strip on phone — Total Portfolio Value and P&L side by side (`grid-cols-2`), Held Assets folded into the P&L card's caption; Transactions count/volumes as a single-line strip. ⚠ interacts with C-16/C-03 only in that the strip must still fit ₺-mode strings.

### MUI-07 · medium · Tap targets under 32px across the phone chrome and cards
- Evidence: header icon buttons 28×28 (`components/layout/Header.tsx:50-52` `size="icon-sm"`, same for ThemeToggle and UserMenu); Portfolio card "Add Tx" 72×24 (`components/portfolio/PortfolioRow.tsx:330` `size="xs"`); row-menu items 120×28 (`shots/ui/P-45-tx-row-menu.png`); Retirement info icons 12×12; Campaigns "Source" links 54×16 and "Track" 64×24; interest card Edit/Close/Delete 24px high (`shots/ui/P-52-asset-try-earning.png`); hero TWR|MWR 43×24; Retirement Nominal|Real 24px. Bottom tabs (98×56) and the type chips (≥41×26) are fine.
- Why it matters: 28px and 24px controls are half the 44pt guideline; the privacy eye and the per-card add-transaction shortcut are the two controls most likely to be mis-tapped on a phone.
- Proposed change: on touch/`< md`, bump icon buttons to `size="icon"` (36–40px) and give text buttons `min-h-10`; the card "Add Tx" becomes a 40px ghost button or moves into a card-level action; dropdown items `py-2.5`. Preserve desktop density.

### MUI-08 · medium · Hover-only affordances have no touch equivalent (clears the deferred consensus item)
- Evidence: tapping the interest badge on a Portfolio card produces no tooltip (`[role=tooltip]` count 0 after tap; `shots/ui/P-34-pf-interest-badge-tap.png`); `components/interest/InterestBadge.tsx:41-78` (Tooltip/TooltipTrigger). The "XIRR"/"MWR" hints are `title=` attributes (`components/dashboard/DashboardHero.tsx:486,606`, `components/portfolio/PortfolioSummaryBar.tsx:84`) — never shown on touch.
- Why it matters: the badge's "ends in 4 days" detail and the MWR/XIRR explainers exist only for mouse users; on the phone the badge is a mystery amber "% 39.5%" pill.
- Proposed change: badge → `Popover` on tap (or link to the asset's Earning section, which already renders the full status); hints → render the one-line hint as visible `text-xs text-muted-foreground` under the figure on `< md`, or make the label a tap-to-toggle. ⚠ interacts with C-15/UX-06: the shared "MWR" micro-label should carry a tap-reachable hint, not `title=`.

### MUI-09 · medium · Asset Detail chart at 390px — two y-axes leave a ~197px plot; controls take two rows of solid pills
- Evidence: `shots/ui/10-asset-aapl-fold.png`/`P-50-asset-chart-tap.png` (left axis "₺160k"/"$3.2k" + right axis "$330" consume ~145px of the 342px card width; plot spans x≈97→293 CSS px; x-axis labels "Jul 12 Sep 9 Nov 9 Jan 9 Mar 8 May 11 Jul 9 Sep 2" abut). `components/asset-detail/AssetHistoryChart.tsx:99,110` (two `<YAxis>` with default `width`), `:98` (no `minTickGap`). Cost/Price toggles + six range pills wrap to two rows of `bg-primary` (`:67,78`).
- Why it matters: the value history — the reason to open the page on a phone — gets 50 % of the card width.
- Proposed change: on `< md` hide the right (price) axis labels (`hide` or `width={0}`; the tooltip already carries Price), set the left axis `width={44}` with a compact formatter (`3.2k`, `160k`), `minTickGap={48}`. C-15's `minTickGap={40}` is compatible. ⚠ interacts with C-15 (stat promotion): in the phone's 2-column grid the promoted Value / Total return / Today land in rows beside un-promoted Quantity / Unrealized / Realized — mixed tiers in one row; on `< md` order the three promoted stats first (own row(s)) before the rest.

### MUI-10 · medium · ⚠ interacts with C-20 — Retirement age labels overprint completely at 390px; "opposite sides" will not separate them

### MUI-16 · low · Portfolio mobile group header carries no subtotal (clears C-12's deferred item)
- Evidence: `shots/ui/02-portfolio-fold.png` ("Fiat — 3 assets" only), `components/portfolio/PortfolioTable.tsx:77-90` (simplified header: dot, label, count). Desktop shows value + return subtotals.
- Why it matters: C-12 (header-as-cells) has no mobile counterpart because the mobile header shows no figures at all — the "does the group add up" scan is impossible on phone.
- Proposed change: add the group's value (and return in the active mode) right-aligned in the mobile header, `text-sm tabular-nums`; the asset count can move into the label ("Fiat · 3").

Also noted (unranked): the Portfolio nested child cards (TP2 under TRY, USDT under USD) are indented 16px with no other parent/child cue and no collapse chevron on phone (`PortfolioTable.tsx:95-104`); the interest banner "Dismiss" ✕ is 24×24; the Sort select opens upward over the tab bar (fine, but it hides the Portfolio tab); the Value-mode 1M x-axis shows two labels on phone as on desktop; the editor's Import popover works on phone (`P-82`) once the button is visible.

Deferred-to-mobile items from the desktop consensus, disposition: C-02/C-17 → MUI-02 (⚠); C-13 → cleared (mobile cards show price only, no Bought column, no second-line issue); C-12 → MUI-16 (cleared as a header-as-cells item, replaced by a missing-subtotal finding); C-08 → MUI-11 (⚠); C-20 → MUI-10 (⚠); C-15 → MUI-09 note (⚠, low); C-04 → cleared (the card list right-aligns Total and stacks Quantity as label-over-value on the left — acceptable card idiom); C-19 → MUI-13 (⚠); hover-only affordances → MUI-08.

Not reviewed: the on-screen keyboard's effect on the Add dialog (headless Chromium cannot raise it — the focused Quantity field sits at y=447 of 664, so a ~300px keyboard would cover it unless the browser scrolls the dialog; unverified); real safe-area rendering (emulator has no notch — MUI-04 is from CSS evidence); landscape phone; iPhone SE interactions beyond dialog geometry (several taps timed out on 375×568 because the target scrolled under the fixed tab bar — noted, not investigated); dark-mode asset icons on Portfolio (`P-91`) showed three white discs for BIMAS/ETH/SOL that may be lazy-load timing — not claimed.
