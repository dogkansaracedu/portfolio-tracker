### MUX-01 · high · Every dialog on a phone — the footer is unreachable without a blind scroll, and the focused field leaves the screen when the keyboard opens · ⚠ interacts with C-02
- Evidence: `shots/02-add-buy.png` (Buy: dialog 33→631px, content 943px — no footer, no scrollbar); geometry for all eight types: Deposit/Fee/Tax content 717px vs 598px visible (submit top 622 vs dialog bottom 631 — clipped), Sell 873, Withdrawal 787, Dividend/Interest 965, Edit 979; `shots/02-add-keyboard-up.png` (viewport 364px ≈ keyboard open: dialog shrinks to 328px, focused Quantity field at y=456 — off-screen); iPhone SE: 511px visible vs 985px content; Campaigns "Track"/asset "Add position" dialog: Save at y=809 vs dialog bottom 614. `src/components/transactions/AddTransactionModal.tsx:659` (`max-h-[90vh]`, footer inside the scroll region), `src/components/interest/InterestPositionForm.tsx:229` (`max-h-[65vh]`), `src/components/ui/dialog.tsx:56` (centred card, `max-w-[calc(100%-2rem)]`).
- Why it matters: "record the trade I just made" on the phone is tap → fill → hunt for the button; with the keyboard up the user cannot see the field they are typing into.
- Proposed change: C-02's sticky footer is necessary but not sufficient here — inside a 328px dialog it leaves ~200px of form. On `< sm` render the Dialog primitive as a full-height sheet (`inset-0`, `h-[100dvh]`, no centring transform) with a fixed header (title + Close), a scrolling body, and the footer pinned to the bottom; use `dvh` so the sheet follows the keyboard. Doing it in `dialog.tsx` fixes Add/Edit Transaction, Track/Add position and Add Asset at once. Acceptance: for every type at 390×664 and 375×568, submit visible without scrolling; with the viewport at 364px the focused input stays on screen.

### MUX-02 · high · 640–1023px (tablet / landscape phone) — a 240px sidebar plus a table that needs 1,040px: Value, P&L and Alloc are off-screen · ⚠ interacts with C-13
- Evidence: `shots/12-tablet-portfolio.png` (768×1024: sidebar visible, table cut after Quantity, "₺1,0…" truncated); DOM: table 1040px in a 480px wrapper; Transactions 972px in 480px (`shots/12-tablet-tx.png`). `src/components/portfolio/PortfolioTable.tsx:41` (`hidden sm:block` — table from 640px) vs `src/components/layout/Sidebar.tsx:57` (`md:flex` — sidebar from 768px); same pair in `src/components/transactions/TransactionList.tsx:47`.
- Why it matters: an iPad-portrait or landscape-phone user gets the worst of both layouts — the phone cards would show every figure, the table shows none of the ones that matter.
- Proposed change: switch table→cards at `lg` (1024px) instead of `sm`, or keep `sm` but show the sidebar only from `lg`. C-13's 990px budget cannot cover the 480–784px content widths this band produces; whichever breakpoint moves, C-13 should be re-measured at 1024×768 (the new narrowest table).

### MUX-03 · high · Transactions on phone — the log begins one full screen below the fold, and the primary action is at the top
- Evidence: `shots/01-transactions-fold.png` (title, two header buttons, three stacked stat cards fill the viewport; filters and the first row are below); DOM: first transaction card at 756px in a 664px viewport — after 4 date presets, 2 date pickers, 2 selects and 9 type chips. `src/pages/TransactionsPage.tsx:63-79` (buttons → `TransactionSummary` → `TransactionFilters` → `TransactionList`), `src/components/transactions/TransactionSummary.tsx` (`grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-3`).
- Why it matters: "did I record that trade?" costs a full-screen scroll before the first row; "record a trade" starts at the top edge, the hardest place to reach one-handed.
- Proposed change: on `< sm` collapse the three stats into one compact row (count · buy · sell), put the filters behind a "Filters (n)" disclosure that opens with the presets and chips, and keep Add Transaction reachable — a floating button above the tab bar or a sticky row — so the list starts within the first screen.

### MUX-04 · medium · Header on phone — the price-staleness indicator disappears; the five controls are 28px tall
- Evidence: header dump on every route: `["", "Switch to dark mode", "$ USD", "" (36×28), "Account menu"]` — the refresh button carries no text; `shots/01-dashboard-fold.png`. `src/components/prices/PriceRefreshButton.tsx:55` (`<span className="hidden sm:inline">{label}</span>` hides "Updated 2m ago" / "No price data"). Spec `docs/components/05-price-engine.md:105-107` ("surfaced as a 3-level indicator").
- Why it matters: on the device where the user glances most, there is no way to tell a live price from a stale one, and the tap targets are the smallest in the app.
- Proposed change: keep a compact age ("2m", "3h") or a fresh/warning/stale dot on the icon below `sm`; give header buttons a 40px hit area (`size-10` with the icon centred) without changing the visual weight.

### MUX-05 · medium · Hover-only explainers with no touch path — retirement "What is…?" icons (12×12, tooltip), hero XIRR/TWR/MWR `title` hints, and the interest badge that navigates when tapped
- Evidence: `shots/06-retirement-hint-tap.png` (tap on a 12×12 "What is Starting amount?" button opens nothing); hero: tap on the XIRR chip → no popup (`title` attribute only); `shots/03-portfolio-badge-tap.png` — tapping the "39.5%" badge (54×20) navigates to `/assets/…` because it sits inside the card's `<Link>`, and the "Sep 6, 2026 · 4 days left" tooltip never shows. `src/components/retirement/RetirementControls.tsx:30` (`<Info className="size-3" />`), `src/components/interest/InterestBadge.tsx:41-62` (Tooltip), `src/components/portfolio/PortfolioRow.tsx:280-287` (badge inside Link), `src/components/dashboard/DashboardHero.tsx:102-105,606`. Spec `docs/components/13-retirement-planning.md:263-264` ("explainer inline — tooltip or caption").
- Why it matters: the glossary explainers are the app's promise that an advanced term is never left bare; on the phone they do not exist. This resolves the consensus' deferred "hover-only affordances" item into a concrete finding.
- Proposed change: retirement hints → Popover on tap with a ≥40px target (or render the one-liner as a muted caption under the label on `< sm`); XIRR/MWR → the same shared hint as a Popover chip; interest badge → make it a button that stops propagation and opens a Popover with rate · end date · days left, or print that line in the card under the ticker.

### MUX-06 · medium · Three data tables reach the phone untouched, and the column that is the point is the one pushed off-screen
- Evidence: Budget months table 538px in a 326px wrapper — Spent and Savings rate columns off-screen (`shots/07-budget-fold.png`; header right edges 476 / 570 vs 326 visible); Asset Detail "By platform" 475px in 326 — Value clipped to "Val", P&L invisible (`shots/08-asset-BTC-full.png`); Settings › Assets 670px in 356 — Price, Group, Status and the Actions menu off-screen (`shots/01-settings-full.png`). Wrappers scroll (`overflow-x: auto`) but show no cue. `src/components/budget/MonthlyBudgetTable.tsx`, `src/components/asset-detail/AssetPlatformTable.tsx`, `src/components/assets/AssetList.tsx:129`. Spec 09 already mandates cards for the transaction log on narrow screens (`09-transactions-page.md:147`); spec 13:272 "tables follow existing table/card conventions on desktop/mobile"; spec 14 is silent.
- Why it matters: Budget's whole question is "what did I spend?" — that column is the hidden one; on Settings the only way to act on an asset is a menu the user cannot see.
- Proposed change: `< sm` treatment per table — Budget: drop Invested to a second line under Income and keep Month · Income · Spent · Rate visible; By platform: stacked rows (platform line, then quantity · value · P&L); Assets: the card list the Transactions page already uses, with Actions in the card header. Add the "cards on narrow screens" clause to specs 14 and 12.

### MUX-07 · medium · Bottom tab bar ignores the safe area
- Evidence: `src/components/layout/MobileNav.tsx:32` (`fixed bottom-0 … border-t`, height 57px); `grep -rn "safe-area" src` → nothing; `src/components/layout/AppLayout.tsx:76` (`pb-20` on main clears the bar itself but not the inset).
- Why it matters: on every notched iPhone the tab labels sit under the home indicator and the last card hides behind the bar.
- Proposed change: `pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom)]` on the nav and `pb-[calc(5rem+env(safe-area-inset-bottom))]` on main; `viewport-fit=cover` in the viewport meta if absent (not verified on this pass).

### MUX-08 · medium · Touch targets under 40px on the daily-job controls
- Evidence (heights from the DOM): Portfolio card "Add Tx" 72×24 (`src/components/portfolio/PortfolioRow.tsx:330`, `size="xs"`) ×19 cards; transaction card "Actions" 28×28 and its Edit/Delete items 28px (`shots/04-tx-actions-menu.png`); Total/Daily/Platform/Category 28px; hero TWR/MWR 24px, range buttons 28px, benchmark chip 20px; type filter chips 26px; Retirement Save/New/Rename/Set default/Delete 28px; banner "Dismiss" 24px and the banner link 274×16; Budget salary-row delete 28px.
- Why it matters: these are the taps the owner makes every day (add a trade, open a row menu, switch Today/Total); at 24–28px they are mis-taps on a moving bus.
- Proposed change: a `< sm` minimum of 40px hit area for buttons and menu items (padding, not larger glyphs): `size="sm"` for the card action, `min-h-10` on ToggleGroup items and DropdownMenu items, and make the interest banner's whole row the link.

### MUX-09 · medium · Retirement on phone — the answer is ~1,100px down behind a 12-input panel; chart labels collide at 326px · ⚠ interacts with C-20
- Evidence: `shots/01-retirement-full.png` (12 stacked full-width inputs and a two-row action bar before the Plan tabs; "RetiremenEagle60retirement: 62" overprint; y-axis −$20M). `src/components/retirement/ScenarioPanel.tsx`, `src/components/retirement/PlanChart.tsx:168-190`.

### MUX-10 · medium · Transaction editor on phone — a 913px spreadsheet in a 390px viewport under a three-row header · ⚠ interacts with C-19
