## 1. Turkish-resident-friendly exchanges and their regulatory standing

The Capital Markets Board (CMB/SPK) now requires every “Crypto Asset Service
Provider” (CASP) that offers services to Turkish residents to hold a CMB
licence. The board publishes an operating list that is the de-facto whitelist
for legal platforms. Entities that appear on that list are considered compliant;
those that do not are blocked and their operators face criminal penalties of
3-12 years imprisonment[1].

- **Binance TR** - operates a Turkish-language site, accepts TRY deposits and is
  on the CMB operating list as of mid-2026.
- **BtcTurk** - Turkey’s first local exchange, fully licensed by the CMB and
  listed on the operating register[1].
- **Paribu** - CMB-licensed, offers Turkish-language support and TRY
  on-ramps[2].
- **OKX TR** - received a CMB licence in the 2025 expansion and now provides TRY
  deposits via FAST, Havale and EFT[3].
- **Bybit TR** - listed on the CMB operating list, runs a Turkish portal and
  supports TRY funding through local bank transfers[4].
- **KuCoin, Gate.io, Bitexen, Bitlo** - all added to the CMB list in 2025 and
  therefore operate legally for Turkish users.

All of the above must comply with AML rules set out in Law No. 5549 and are
“obliged parties” under MASAK, meaning they must collect KYC data, monitor
transactions and report suspicious activity[1].

## 2. Staking-type and reward-earning products available to Turkish residents

### 2.1 Binance TR - “Binance Earn”

- **Product types:** Flexible Savings, Locked Savings, Staking, Launchpool,
  Dual-Investment.
- **Supported assets:** Major coins (BTC, ETH, BNB, USDT, USDC) and many
  alt-coins; Launchpool adds new tokens periodically.
- **Minimum stake:** From $1 (≈ ≈ 20 TRY) for flexible products; locked products
  often require 10 USDT or the equivalent[5].
- **Lock-up:** Flexible products are withdrawable any time; locked terms range
  from 30 days to 90 days. Early withdrawal forfeits all earned rewards.
- **Reward rates:** Flexible APY 3-20 % (varies by asset); locked APY up to 20 %
  for BNB and up to 28.9 % for select promotional tokens[5].
- **Fees:** No explicit management fee; the displayed APR already incorporates
  any platform cost. Withdrawal of the principal is free, but network fees apply
  when moving assets off-chain[5].
- **Promotions:** Periodic “Launchpool” campaigns give extra token rewards; no
  extra bonus on the base Earn products.

### 2.2 Bybit TR - “Bybit Earn”

- **Product types:** Flexible Savings, Locked Savings, ETH-2.0 staking pool, SOL
  liquid-staking, Dual-Investment, high-reward promotional USDT staking.
- **Supported assets:** USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH, SOL, APT, plus a rotating list of
  DeFi tokens.
- **Minimum stake:** 1 USDT for flexible savings; 10 USDT for most locked
  products; 0.01 ETH for the ETH-2.0 pool[4].
- **Lock-up:** Flexible products have no lock-up. Locked Savings are typically
  30 days; promotional USDT “666 % APR” runs for 3 days and cannot be withdrawn
  early[6].
- **Reward rates:** Flexible APY 4-6 % on USDT, 0.5-1.5 % on BTC, 1-2 % on
  ETH[7]. Locked USDT 6.5-8.5 % for 30-day term; the limited-time 666 % APR
  promotion for new users.
- **Fees:** No subscription fee; network withdrawal fees apply. Early withdrawal
  of locked products is not allowed and results in loss of accrued interest[4].
- **Promotions:** Besides the 666 % APR USDT sprint, Bybit runs occasional
  “dual-investment” offers with APY 8-50 % for short-term structured
  products[7].

### 2.3 OKX TR - “OKX Earn”

- **Product types:** Flexible Savings, Fixed Savings, Staking (on-chain
  delegated), Liquid-staking tokens (e.g., BETH, OKSOL).
- **Supported assets:** Over 100 tokens, including ETH, SOL, USDT, USDC, BTC,
  plus liquid-staking derivatives.
- **Minimum stake:** 10 USDT (or equivalent) for flexible savings; 10-100 USDT
  for fixed terms; 0.01 ETH for ETH staking[8].
- **Lock-up:** Flexible savings withdrawable anytime; fixed terms of 15, 30 or
  90 days; liquid-staking tokens remain tradable while earning rewards.
- **Reward rates:** Flexible APY 1-6 % (higher for stablecoins); Fixed Savings
  7-9 % for 30-day USDC; Staking yields follow on-chain rates (e.g., ETH ≈ 3 %
  APY)[8].
- **Fees:** No subscription fee; standard network withdrawal fees apply. Early
  exit from fixed products forfeits the promised APY but returns the principal.
- **Promotions:** New-user welcome bonuses (e.g., boosted APY for the first 7
  days) are announced sporadically in the app[8].

### 2.4 Paribu - “Paribu Staking & Earn”

- **Product types:** Flexible staking, Fixed-term staking pools, “Earn”
  (interest-bearing) accounts for both crypto and Turkish Lira.
- **Supported assets:** ETH, SOL, AVAX, CHZ, plus TRY-denominated pools.
- **Minimum stake:** Typically 0.01 ETH or the equivalent in TRY for Lira
  pools[9].
- **Lock-up:** Flexible pools have no lock-up; fixed pools range from 7 days to
  30 days. Early withdrawal from fixed pools incurs a penalty of forfeiting the
  accrued reward.
- **Reward rates:** Daily reward rates translate to APY of roughly 5-12 %
  depending on the asset and term; TRY-staking offers a stable APY around 8-10
  %[10].
- **Fees:** No platform-level fee; only blockchain network fees on withdrawals.
- **Promotions:** Occasionally Paribu runs “extra % reward” campaigns for
  specific assets (e.g., +1 % on staking during a month) announced on the app.

### 2.5 KuCoin - “KuCoin Earn”

- **Product types:** Fixed-term staking, Flexible staking, Soft-staking
  (interest without locking), and promotional “high-return” offers.
- **Supported assets:** ETH, SOL, KCS, plus dozens of alt-coins; promotional
  rates can exceed 100 % APR for short-term campaigns (e.g., MELOS 101 %
  APR)[11].
- **Minimum stake:** Usually 10 USDT or equivalent; some promotions start at 5
  USDT.
- **Lock-up:** Fixed terms from 7 days to 30 days; flexible staking has no
  lock-up. Early withdrawal from fixed terms loses the interest.
- **Reward rates:** Flexible APY 1-5 %; Fixed APY up to 30 % for popular tokens;
  promotional spikes (e.g., 101 % APR) for limited periods[11].
- **Fees:** No management fee; network fees apply on withdrawal.

### 2.6 Gate.io - “Gate Earn”

- **Product types:** Flexible Savings, Fixed Savings, Staking, Dual-Investment,
  “HODL & Earn”.
- **Supported assets:** Over 2 000 tokens, including ETH, SOL, USDT, BTC, and
  many DeFi tokens.
- **Minimum stake:** 10 USDT for most products; some promotional assets accept 1
  USDT.
- **Lock-up:** Flexible products are withdrawable any time; fixed terms of 15-90
  days. Early exit forfeits accrued interest.
- **Reward rates:** Flexible APY 2-6 %; Fixed APY 7-30 % depending on the asset;
  dual-investment can reach 50 % APY for short-term structured products[12].
- **Fees:** No platform fee; standard blockchain withdrawal fees.

### 2.7 Bitexen - “Bitexen Staking”

- **Product types:** Token-specific staking pools (e.g., EXEN, ETH, SOL) and
  “Earn” savings for TRY.
- **Supported assets:** EXEN native token, ETH, SOL, plus a handful of popular
  coins.
- **Minimum stake:** 0.01 ETH or equivalent; TRY-staking requires at least 10
  TRY.
- **Lock-up:** Fixed terms of 7-30 days; flexible pool available for EXEN with
  no lock-up. Early withdrawal from fixed pools loses the reward.
- **Reward rates:** APY roughly 5-12 % for crypto pools; TRY-staking around 8-10
  % (derived from the platform’s public fee schedule).
- **Fees:** No staking fee; network fees on withdrawals.

## 3. Deposit and withdrawal mechanics for Turkish users

All CMB-licensed exchanges must allow Turkish Lira (TRY) on-ramps through the
domestic banking infrastructure. The most common channels are:

- **FAST** - instant 24/7 transfers up to 100 000 TRY; supported by Binance TR,
  Bybit TR, OKX TR, and others[3].
- **Havale** - same-bank intra-bank transfers; limits vary by bank but are
  generally free of charge[3].
- **EFT** - inter-bank transfers processed during business hours (09:00-16:45);
  used for amounts above FAST limits or for banks that do not support FAST[3].
- **Card purchases** - accepted by Binance TR and Bybit TR for instant TRY
  credit, though the card-issued funds cannot be used for withdrawals and may
  incur a small fee[13].

Withdrawal limits are generally set per-day and per-month (e.g., 20 million TRY
daily for individuals on OKX TR) and are enforced to meet AML requirements[3].

All platforms listed provide Turkish-language customer support via live chat,
phone, or email, and the support pages are fully localized (e.g., Paribu,
BtcTurk, OKX TR)[2].

## 4. Tax-reporting and compliance considerations

Turkish tax law treats crypto-related income as taxable under the general
income-tax regime. Individuals must declare any realized gains on the annual
“Yıllık Beyanname” (income-tax return) regardless of size; there is no specific
exemption threshold[14]. The applicable rate is progressive from 15 % to 40
%[14].

For corporate entities (e.g., a limited-liability company that runs a staking
operation) profits are subject to a flat 25 % corporate tax and must follow FIFO
accounting[15].

Since July 2024, CASPs are “obliged parties” under MASAK and must withhold a 10
% tax on net crypto-sale gains and remit it to the Revenue Administration
(GİB)[1]. The CMB-licensed exchanges automatically perform this withholding and
report transaction data to the tax authority, which means Turkish users of
licensed platforms do not need to file separate withholding statements for those
trades[16].

However, earnings from **staking, DeFi yields, or interest-bearing “savings”
accounts** are treated as **other income** and must be reported separately. The
tax administration now receives detailed transaction logs from the exchanges
(via the DAC8-style reporting introduced in 2026)[14], so non-declaration can
trigger penalties ranging from 10 % to 40 % of the unpaid tax plus interest[14].

For Turkish residents using foreign-registered exchanges that serve Turkish
users, the same AML and tax-reporting obligations apply if the platform has a
“substantial Turkish market presence” (e.g., Turkish-language UI, local
marketing, or processing TRY)[17]. Failure to comply can lead to criminal
charges under the Turkish Criminal Code (3-12 years imprisonment)[1].

### Quick reference checklist for Turkish stakers

1. **Choose a CMB-licensed exchange** (Binance TR, BtcTurk, Paribu, OKX TR,
   Bybit TR, KuCoin, Gate.io, Bitexen, etc.).
2. **Verify the staking product** (flexible vs. locked, token, APY, minimum,
   lock-up, early-withdrawal penalty).
3. **Fund the account in TRY** using FAST, Havale or EFT; ensure the bank
   account name matches the verified KYC details.
4. **Stake the desired amount** and record the transaction date, amount, and APY
   for tax purposes.
5. **Monitor the reward distribution** (daily, weekly, or at maturity) and keep
   the statements for the annual tax filing.
6. **Report any realized gains** (selling the earned tokens) on the Yıllık
   Beyanname; staking rewards are reported as other income.

By following the regulatory framework and using the licensed platforms listed
above, Turkish residents can safely access a broad spectrum of staking-type
campaigns while remaining compliant with AML, CMB licensing, and tax
obligations.
